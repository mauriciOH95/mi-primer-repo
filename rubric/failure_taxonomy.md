# Failure Taxonomy

Use these tags to identify *why* a voice sample failed. Tags complement the numeric scores and make error analysis more actionable.

## Pronunciation
- `mispronunciation`
- `incorrect_stress`
- `phoneme_substitution`
- `number_reading_error`
- `date_time_reading_error`
- `proper_noun_error`
- `foreign_word_error`

## Prosody
- `flat_intonation`
- `exaggerated_intonation`
- `incorrect_emphasis`
- `question_intonation_error`
- `phrasing_error`

## Rhythm & pace
- `pace_too_slow`
- `pace_too_fast`
- `unnatural_pause`
- `missing_pause`
- `word_boundary_error`
- `rhythm_instability`

## Voice consistency
- `voice_shift`
- `pitch_instability`
- `loudness_shift`
- `timbre_shift`
- `style_drift`
- `accent_drift`

## Audio quality
- `audio_distortion`
- `clipping`
- `metallic_artifact`
- `glitch`
- `dropout`
- `background_artifact`
- `unnatural_breathing`

## Holistic naturalness
- `robotic_delivery`
- `overly_synthetic`
- `emotion_mismatch`
- `context_inappropriate_delivery`

## Severity guideline

- **Minor:** noticeable defect with little effect on usability.
- **Major:** clearly affects naturalness, intelligibility, or perceived quality.
- **Critical:** makes the sample unusable or changes the intended linguistic content.
